Test 1 — Header fields

Voucher type: Sales Invoice

Preconditions:
- User is logged into the Rachis tenant
- At least one Customer exists with known GST state
- At least one Item exists in the Item master with Rate and GST% set
Test data:
- Customer: (to be selected from tenant)
- Invoice date: current date
- Due date: 30 days from invoice date
- Payment terms: Net 30 (optional, can be left blank)

Steps

  1. Open a new Sales Invoice voucher
  2. Verify Top block shows: Customer field, Invoice date, Due date, Place of supply, Payment terms
  3. Verify Title area shows voucher type and voucher number
  4. Click "▸ More details" — reveal Salesperson / PO fields
  5. Select a Customer whose GST state is known
  6. Verify Place of supply shows state name with 2-digit code (e.g. "Tamil Nadu (33)")
  7. Verify Payment terms can be left blank and voucher still saves

Expected result

  • All header fields present and functional
  • Customer selection populates Place of supply with state name and code
  • Payment terms optional — voucher saves even when blank
  • "More details" disclosure reveals secondary fields
