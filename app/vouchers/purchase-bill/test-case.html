Test 1 — Header fields

Voucher type: Purchase Bill

Preconditions:
- User is logged into the Rachis tenant
- At least one Vendor/Supplier exists in the tenant
- At least one Item exists in the Item master with Rate and GST% set
Test data:
- Vendor: (to be selected from tenant)
- Invoice date: current date
- Due date: 30 days from invoice date
- Payment terms: Net 30 (optional, can be left blank)

Steps

  1. Open a new Purchase Bill voucher
  2. Verify Top block shows: Vendor field, Invoice date, Due date, Place of supply, Payment terms — Payment terms and the date fields sit opposite the Vendor field
  3. Verify Title area shows the voucher type on one line and the voucher number just below it
  4. Click/expand "▸ More details" link/disclosure — reveal PO number and similar secondary fields
  5. Select a Vendor whose GST state is known
  6. Verify Place of supply shows the state name with its 2-digit code, e.g. "Kerala (32)"
  7. Verify Payment terms field can be left blank and the voucher still saves

Expected result

  • All header fields present and functional
  • Vendor selection populates Place of supply with state name and code
  • Payment terms are optional — voucher saves even when blank
  • "More details" disclosure reveals secondary fields (PO number, etc.)
