Test 0 — Sign in

Voucher type: none — this is the door every other case walks through

Preconditions:
- A tenant user exists with a known email and password
- Credentials are held in .env as RACHIS_USERNAME and RACHIS_PASSWORD, never in this file
Test data:
- Email: from .env
- Password: from .env
- Remember me: left unchecked unless the run is meant to outlive itself

This case is the reason test.html lists login first. Nothing below the header block, no voucher route, and no line item can be reached until this passes, so it is checked on its own before the voucher phases begin. The driver performs it automatically from .env — a human is never asked to type a username or password.

Steps

  1. Open /login/
  2. Verify the page identifies itself: heading "Sign in to Rachis Books", the "SECURE ACCESS" marker, and "STEP 1 OF 1 — CREDENTIALS"
  3. Verify the production marker reads PRODUCTION · AP-SOUTH-1, so a run against the wrong environment is visible before any data is touched
  4. Verify exactly two credential fields: Email Address (type=email, autocomplete email) and Password (type=password, autocomplete current-password)
  5. Verify "Show password" toggles the password field's masking, and that it starts masked
  6. Verify "Keep me signed in for 30 days" starts unchecked
  7. Fill Email Address and Password from .env
  8. Submit with the "SIGN IN" button
  9. Verify the form POSTs to /login/?qaction=… and that a hidden redirect_to field is carried with it
  10. Verify the browser is redirected off /login/ and lands on /dashboard/
  11. Verify the signed-in header shows the company ("Ecomsense") and fiscal period ("FY 2025 ▾") switchers, confirming which tenant the run is inside
  12. Verify the driver reports "ok" and moves to step 1 without pausing

Failure behaviour

A deliberately wrong password is not attempted here by default. The tenant footer states that all sign-in events are audited and that sessions expire after 8 hours of inactivity. Firing a known-bad credential at a production system risks a lockout and leaves an audit entry that looks like an attack. If the negative case is wanted, a human decides it explicitly and once — it is not part of an ordinary run.

Expected if it is ever run: the page stays on /login/ and shows "The email or password is incorrect. Please try again." Nothing is redirected, and no voucher screen is reached.

Expected result

  • The login form is present with both credential fields and a working Show password toggle
  • Submit posts to /login/?qaction=… and redirects to /dashboard/
  • The signed-in header names the company and fiscal period, so the run's target tenant is never in doubt
  • The driver continues to the voucher cases without any human typing a credential

Out of scope

  • Account creation (/signup) and password reset (/forgot-password) — both exist as links and are not exercised
  • The Terms of Service and Privacy Policy links
  • Session expiry mid-run — noted as an 8 hour inactivity limit, not tested
