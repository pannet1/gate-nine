Exclusions — do not test, do not report

Carried verbatim from the brief. Nothing below is a failure in this run.

  • ₹-per-unit vs ₹-on-whole-line line-discount mode — not built
  • Any GST / e-invoice treatment of a discounted invoice — frozen pending consultant review
  • TCS and cess showing zero
  • Reverse Charge (RCM) purchases with no input-tax-credit leg
  • GST on advance receipts showing zero — required by law for goods
  • Sales Order, Proforma Invoice, Journal Voucher, Stock Transfer, Expense Voucher, Delivery Challan, Work Order, Job Card — none exist in this version
  • GRN draft editing behaving differently from other vouchers (Vendor/PO locked)
  • Correcting a posted Receipt or Vendor Payment amount — only allocation may change
  • Changing quantities on a posted GDN — restricted by design; use reversal/reissue
  • Missing A4/A5 or print-template choice where Settings does not expose them
  • Place of supply showing a bare 2-digit code instead of a state name — known gap, already backlogged
